Controls

  • Hold the iPhone in landscape and drag on the left side to move.
  • The pilot auto-aims and auto-fires; drag on the right side for precision focus aim.
  • Use Dash to cross danger safely and Pulsebreak when the active ability is ready.
  • Pause and mute stay available from the upper-right safe area.

Quick checks

  1. Rotate the iPhone to landscape; active play intentionally pauses in portrait.
  2. Close and reopen the app if iOS interrupted it during an update.
  3. Use Restart run from the pause or recap screen to begin a clean sortie.
  4. Turn on Reduce Motion in iOS if screen shake is uncomfortable.

Current release
