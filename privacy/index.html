Support Privacy

Effective August 15, 2026

Your run stays yours.

Pulsebreak does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from the app. It has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or purchase system.

Data in the app

Gameplay runs locally on your device. Pulsebreak does not collect identifiers, gameplay activity, diagnostics, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Local storage

The app may save preferences such as mute state on your device. This information is not sent to us and is removed when iOS removes the app's local data.

Website delivery

The public Pulsebreak website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or marketing cookies. Its hosting provider may process basic connection information, such as an IP address and request time, only to deliver and secure the site.

Learn how the hosting provider handles that information in GitHub's privacy statement .

Support messages

If you email support, we use the information you choose to send only to answer and resolve your request. Support email is not linked to app activity or used for advertising.

You can ask us to delete a support conversation, subject to any legal retention obligation.

Children

Pulsebreak does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app does not include chat, user-generated content, or social features.

Changes

If a future release changes how data is handled, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that release. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
